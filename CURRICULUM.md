# ICU Atlas curriculum inventory

This is the inventory of authored lessons, not a claim of equivalence to an AACN course. Every lesson has a mechanism, recognition cues, nursing response, reassessment, a misconception, a worked case, teach-back and four application questions. The app links the readings at lesson level.

Total: 125 lessons, 500 quiz cases, 12 pathways.
